## Organizing a competition

The path most organizers follow, in order. Each step is a group of pages in the
[Organizer](/organizer/) section.

1. [Plan your competition](/organizer/plan/): what is required of you, and the shortest way to a working event
2. [Set up the event](/organizer/event/): details, users and apps, floors, screens, sponsors
3. [Classes, rounds and judges](/organizer/classes-rounds-judges/): what is danced, who judges it, and the schedule
4. [Registration and sales](/organizer/registration-and-sales/): entries, e-mails, tickets and the door
5. [Rehearsal and event day](/organizer/event-day/): prove it before the day, then run it
6. [After the event](/organizer/after/): results, points, exports and diplomas
7. [Federations and seasons](/organizer/federations-and-seasons/): running under a federation, and what changes between seasons
