{%- comment -%}
The pages under one page, in sidebar order, as llms.txt lines. Calls itself for each page's
own children. `children` is overwritten by the inner call, which is safe: the loop already
holds its list.

  parent    the title of the page whose children are listed
  reversed  that page's child_nav_order
{%- endcomment -%}
{%- assign children = site.pages | where: "parent", include.parent | sort: "nav_order" -%}
{%- if include.reversed == "reversed" or include.reversed == "desc" -%}
{%- assign children = children | reverse -%}
{%- endif -%}
{%- for child in children %}
- [{{ child.title }}]({{ site.url }}{{ child.url }}): {{ child.description }}
{%- include llms_children.txt parent=child.title reversed=child.child_nav_order -%}
{%- endfor -%}
