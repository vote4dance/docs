# Federation

For federation staff: the people who set a federation's structure and rules, approve clubs, issue licenses and publish rankings in Vote4Dance. If that is not you, find your section on [Start here](/getting-started/).

## Set up your federation

Who has access, and the federation's own details. [Set up your federation](/federation/setup/) gives the order to do things in when the federation is new.

- [Federation Staff Roles](/federation/staff-roles/): add staff and give each person the right role.
- [Federation settings](/federation/settings/): the federation's details, Stripe, the public page, API keys and integrations.

## Clubs and people

[Clubs and people](/federation/clubs-and-people/): the clubs in your federation and the people in them.

- [Members](/federation/members/): competitors, dancers, clubs, class history and the enforcement log.
- [Approving clubs](/federation/joining-a-federation/): approve a club that applies, and decide whether it may issue licenses.
- [Club transfers](/federation/club-transfers/): move a dancer or a competitor from one club to another.
- [When dancers, clubs or staff leave](/federation/retiring/): retire a membership, remove a club or remove a staff member.

## Structure and rules

[Structure and rules](/federation/structure-and-rules/): what your federation dances, and by which rules.

- [Divisions and disciplines](/federation/divisions/): divisions, disciplines, categories, age groups, class levels and classes.
- [Judging](/federation/judging/): the judging setups that rounds can use.
- [Federation Rules](/federation-rules/): the rules Vote4Dance enforces automatically, with a page for each area:
  - [Setting federation rules](/federation-rules/editor/): the editor every level shares.
  - [How the round setup works](/federation-rules/reference/): how a class's rounds are generated.
  - [Adding and editing round setup rules](/federation-rules/round-setup-rules/): the rules list of a discipline and the rule editor.
  - [Checking the round setup](/federation-rules/round-setup-checks/): findings in the rules, and why a class gets the rounds it gets.
  - [Merging and linking round setup rules](/federation-rules/round-setup-merging/): one rule instead of copies, in a discipline and across disciplines.
  - [Setting the default ladder](/federation-rules/default-ladder/): the ladder for classes no other rule gives one.
  - [Round setup conditions, actions and settings](/federation-rules/round-setup-settings/): what each one matches, does or controls.
  - [Age category rules](/federation-rules/age-category-rules/): which age category a solo, duo or group belongs to.
  - [Battle round setup](/federation-rules/battle-class-rules/) and [Battle sub-rounds](/federation-rules/battle-sub-rounds/): how battles are built.
  - [Round templates](/federation-rules/round-setups/): describe a ladder of rounds as a table.
  - [Own music](/federation-rules/own-music/): classes where dancers upload their own track.
  - [What your rules do at event registration](/federation/registration-for-event/): what is checked when someone registers, and where you set it.
- [Age moves](/federation/age-moves/): move competitors up an age group at the start of a season.

## Federation Licenses

[Federation Licenses](/federation-licenses/): what you offer, who applies, and how you approve.

- [Setting up licenses](/federation/getting-a-license/): license items, payment, class mappings and a test of each route.
- [Federation admin](/federation-licenses/federation-admin/): the Licenses screen, field by field: issue, review, approve and correct licenses.
- [Starting a new license season](/federation/renew-license/): what to check and tell people at each season rollover.

## Points and rankings

[Points and rankings](/federation/points-and-rankings/): which of the four you need.

- [Promotion points](/federation/progression/): points that move competitors up a class ladder.
- [Closing a class at points](/federation/class-point-limits/): a points limit after which a team can no longer enter a class level.
- [Entry rules and the season ranking](/federation/season-ranking/): who may enter an event by season points, and the public season ranking.
- [Federation Rankings](/federation-rankings/): rankings built from results with a formula you set.

## Competitions and statistics

- [Competitions](/federation/competitions/): every competition run under your federation, and what a finished one still owes you.
- [Federation statistics](/federation/statistics/): how the season stands in licenses, members, participation and competitions.
