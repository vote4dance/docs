# Results, points and rankings

> **Does this apply to you?** Results apply to every event. Promotion points, season points and rankings only apply if the event runs under a federation that uses them.

After you compete, several things can happen with your result. They are updated at
different times and by different people, which is why one can be visible while another is not.

| | What it is | When it updates | Where you see it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Result** | Your placement in the class at that competition. | When the organizer publishes the round. | The competition's results on vote4dance.com and in the app. |
| **Promotion points** | Points that count towards moving up a class. | When the organizer **closes** the competition. | Your public profile in the app, under **Ranking and progression**. |
| **Season points** | Points that close a class to your team once it has enough of them in one season. Only some federations use them. | When the competition's results are public: when the organizer closes the competition, or earlier if the organizer publishes results while it runs. | The same place. See [Your season points in the app](/dancer/season-points/). |
| **Ranking** | Your position in a federation ranking list, built from several competitions. | When the federation recalculates the ranking: often every night, or when staff refresh it. | `Federation → Ranking` on vote4dance.com. |

Promotion points and season points are two different counts. A federation uses one, both or
neither, so do not expect them to change at the same moment.

You do not create a ranking or apply for one. The federation sets rankings up. Your part is to
compete in the right class, with the right license, as the right couple or team.

## 1. Your result

The organizer publishes results round by round. Once the final is published, your placement is
on the competition's results page. If the competition is over and you see nothing, the results
are not published yet.

## 2. Promotion points and your class

Promotion points are added when the organizer **closes** the competition, not when the results
are published. Closing can happen days after the event.

Season points are counted separately and can show earlier: see
[Your season points in the app](/dancer/season-points/).

- Points belong to the **couple or team**, not to each dancer on their own.
- When the couple reaches the class's points limit, it moves up a class automatically.
  See [Moving up a class](/dancer/class-transfer/).
- The couple is identified by the dancers' **federation IDs** on the entry. A wrong federation ID
  can put your points on a second, duplicate couple. If your points look split, tell your
  federation. See [Your federation ID](/dancer/getting-a-license/#your-federation-id).

The full mechanism is on [Promotion points](/federation/progression/).

## 3. Rankings

A ranking is a list the federation publishes, for example "Season ranking, Adults Standard". It
is calculated from the results of several competitions.

**How to find it:** on vote4dance.com, open `Federation`, pick the federation, open `Ranking`,
then pick the division and the ranking. See [Public Rankings](/federation-rankings/public/).

Each ranking has its own rules, set by the federation:

- **Which classes count.** A ranking only includes the classes the federation has linked to it.
- **Which competitions count.** For example the current season only, or your best three
  results.
- **How it scores.** By placement, by points per placement, or by the judges' scores.
- **License rules.** Some rankings only include dancers with an active license.

Many rankings are recalculated every night, so a result can take a day to appear.

## If something is missing or looks wrong

Work down this list:

| What you see | Most likely reason |
|---|---|
| No result at all | The organizer has not published it yet. |
| Result is there, but no promotion points | The competition is not closed yet. |
| Result is there, but no season points | The competition's results are not public yet. They are at the latest when the organizer closes the competition. |
| Points are shared with a couple you do not recognise | A wrong federation ID on an entry created a duplicate couple. Tell your federation. |
| Result is there, but not in the ranking | The ranking has not been recalculated yet (wait a day), or that competition or class does not count for this ranking. |
| Your ranking position changed with no new competition | An older result left the time window, or federation staff made a correction. |
| You are not in the ranking at all | Your license was not active, or you are looking in the wrong division. |
| An old result shows your name as text only, not linked to you | Claim it in the app: on **Profile**, tap **Claim past results**, or use the dancer page that opens when you tap the name. A result can only be claimed once the competition has closed. The claim joins the couple that already has the result, so its points and ranking follow. Once every place in the team is claimed, it is shown by its members' names, unless someone chose a name for it; a group or formation keeps its own name. A result the archive spells differently from your search shows **Spelling differs** and starts unticked. Tick it only if it's yours: an accent can be all that separates two dancers. A result entered with a federation ID that isn't yours, often a number from the organizer's own system, shows **Check this is you** and also starts unticked. (The app calls the federation ID your member number here.) You can claim it as long as no other dancer holds that number, and its birth year, if it has one, is the one on your account. |

If none of these explains it, contact your federation. Include the competition, the class and
the name of the ranking.
