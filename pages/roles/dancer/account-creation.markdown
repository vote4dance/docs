# Creating your account

Your account holds your identity, your federation affiliation, your licenses, your registrations and your rankings in Vote4Dance. You create it yourself, and it is personal, not shared.

## Where to go

Go to **https://vote4dance.com** and choose **Sign in / Create account** at the top of the page. There is no separate sign-up and no password to invent: you create your account with your e-mail address and a code, see [Signing in](/signing-in/).

## Before you start

Gather these details:

- Your first and last name as your **federation or event organizer knows them**, not a nickname
- Your email address, and access to its inbox: Vote4Dance sends you a code there
- The name of the school or club you will join, if you know it. You can join it later

## One account per dancer

**Every dancer needs their own account**, in their own name and with their own birth date. If you
are a parent, add each child under [Family](/dancer/family/): you get an account for each child that
you open from your own, with no second inbox to keep track of.

Each account needs its own email address, because the email is the sign-in name. Two children need
two different addresses. They can belong to a parent; they just cannot be shared between accounts.

## Step-by-step: Create your account

### Step 1: Start the signup

1. Go to https://vote4dance.com and choose **Sign in / Create account**
2. Type your **e-mail** and press **Continue**. Use your own permanent address: it is your sign-in name, and where registration confirmations and the check-in QR code go.
3. Type the 6-digit code that Vote4Dance mails to that address. It works for 10 minutes.
4. Fill in the short form that follows a new address:

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Firstname** | Letters, spaces, hyphens and apostrophes only. |
| **Lastname** | Same. Use the name your federation and organizers know you by, not a nickname: this is how you are found in entry lists. |
| **Nationality** | Required. |
| **I accept the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy** | Required. |

5. Press **Create account**

You are **signed in immediately**. There is **no password to invent**. If you already have an account with that e-mail address, the code signs you in to it, so you never end up with two accounts by mistake. The steps are described in full, with screenshots, on [Signing in](/signing-in/).

After signing in on a phone or computer that supports it, Vote4Dance offers to save a **passkey**, so your face or fingerprint signs you in next time. See [Save a passkey](/signing-in/#save-a-passkey).

There is no birth date, club or license on this form. Those come next.

### Step 2: Complete your profile

1. Open your name at the top right and choose **My account**, then **Settings**.
2. Under **User details**, fill in:
   - **Birthdate**: your birth date. Classes depend on age, and a license cannot be applied for without it
   - **Gender**
   - **Nationality**
3. Press **Save changes**.

**Join organization** is refused with "Missing profile information" until all three are
filled in, so do this before you look for your club. Dancers who already had an account should
check these too.

Your birth date, your e-mail address and your phone number are not shown on your public dancer
page. What other people can see of an account is listed under
[What other people can see](/dancer/family/#what-other-people-can-see).

## What your account looks like after creation

- You can sign in anytime at vote4dance.com with your email and a code (or a passkey, if you saved one)
- Your name appears in the top-right corner after signing in
- **My account** holds your details, your **Vote4Dance ID** card (the QR code the check-in desk scans) and your profile photo
- If your federation requires a license, you need one before you can register for its events. Add your birth date first: licenses and age groups need it.

## Choosing your language

Vote4Dance is in English, Swedish, Spanish, Finnish, Norwegian (Bokmål), Dutch, German and French.
The Vote4Dance app has all of them except Spanish.

- **On the web**, until you pick a language your browser's language list decides. To pick one,
  use the globe at the top of the page, next to your language's code (for example **EN**). When you are signed in, your choice is
  saved to your account, so it follows you to other devices. You can also change it under
  **My account** → **Settings** → **Language**.
- **In the Vote4Dance app**, the first language in your phone's language list that Vote4Dance has is
  used. You can pick another on the first screen, before you sign in, or under **Settings** → **Language**.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What to do |
|---|---|
| **You already have an account, but are not sure which email it used** | Try the addresses you may have used. A code sent to the right one signs you in. Do not create a second account with another email: your results, licenses and registrations would be split across two accounts. |
| **The code does not arrive** | Look in Spam, wait for **Send again** and use it once, and check the address. See [If something goes wrong](/signing-in/#if-something-goes-wrong). |
| **Your name is misspelled** | Sign in, open **My account** → **Settings**, correct the name and press **Save changes**. Do this before you join a club or apply for a license. |
| **You want to use another e-mail address** | Change it under **My account** → **Settings**: see [Change your e-mail address](/signing-in/#change-your-e-mail-address). Do not create a new account with the new address. |
| **An error says the captcha is blocked** | A browser privacy setting or ad blocker is blocking it. Allow it for vote4dance.com, or try another browser. |
| **Your school, club or organizer cannot find you later** | You are probably signed in with a different account than the one they know. |

## Check before you go on

- You can sign out and sign back in.
- Your name is spelled the way your federation knows it.
- You have only **one** Vote4Dance account.
