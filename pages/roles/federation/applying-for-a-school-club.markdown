# How clubs work with your federation

Clubs are where most of your dancers come from, and they do much of the licence work for you. This
page explains what a club can do in your federation, and what you decide for each club.

## What you decide per club

| Decision | Where | What it controls |
|---|---|---|
| **Approved** or **pending** | The club's page, `Federation → Members → Organizations` | Until you approve a club, no licence naming that club can be issued. |
| **Licence issuer** or not | The same page, **Allow license issuing** | Whether the club can hold licences for items whose organization mode is `Required`. |

Both need the **Administrator** role. How a club applies and how you approve it:
[Joining a federation](/federation/joining-a-federation/).

## What you decide per licence item

Whether a licence goes through a club is set on each **licence item**, not per club:

| Organization mode | Means |
|---|---|
| `Not allowed` | No club on the licence. Dancers apply themselves, or you issue it. |
| `Optional` | Either route. |
| `Required` | The licence must name a club, and the club must be an approved issuer. |

You also decide whether members may apply for an item themselves. Whether clubs must approve
their members' applications first is your choice as well. See
[Getting a license model in place](/federation/getting-a-license/).

## What a club does

With the right club roles, a club:

- adds its members and approves those who apply to join
- applies for licences for its members, one at a time, and approves what members apply for
  themselves
- pays for the licences the club pays for
- creates and registers its competitors for events
- confirms its dancers at check-in on competition day

The club's own guide is under [School/Club](/school-club/).

## Which club a dancer represents

Representation comes from **licences**, not club membership. A competitor represents the club that
holds the most active dancer licences among its dancers. So when a dancer moves club, you reissue
the licence to the new club. See [Club transfers](/federation/club-transfers/).

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| A club's dancers are all stuck on Pending licences | The club is not approved yet. |
| A club cannot apply for a licence that needs a club | The club is not a licence issuer, or the item's organization mode is `Not allowed`. |
| Two profiles exist for one club | Pick one. Moving licences and teams to it is done from [Club transfers](/federation/club-transfers/). |
