# Approving clubs

Clubs are where most of your dancers come from, and they do much of the license work for you. A
club applies to your federation, you approve it, and you decide whether it may issue licenses. This
page covers those decisions and what a club can do once it is in.

## What you are actually doing

Clubs apply to you from their own side (`Organization → Administration → Federation` →
**Apply**), and land on `Federation → Members → Organizations` as **pending**. You have two separate decisions to make about each one:

| Decision | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Approve organization** | The club becomes **Approved**. Until you do, no license naming that club can be issued. The block is shown on the license queue as "Approve the club first", and bulk approvals skip those licenses rather than forcing them through. |
| **Allow license issuing** | A separate switch. Approved but without it, "This organization is linked to the federation but cannot issue licenses"; with it, the club "can issue or manage federation licenses where an organization is required". |

Both are buttons on the club's page (**Approve organization**, **Allow license issuing**), and
both need the **Manager** role or higher. Removing a club from the federation needs an
Administrator. See
[staff roles](/federation/staff-roles/).

A club belongs to one federation at a time, so a club moving to you has to detach from its
previous federation first.

**Approve clubs before the season's license rush.** A pending club silently blocks every license
its dancers apply for, and neither the dancers nor the club always work out why.

## Before you approve a club

Vote4Dance has no application form: the club only presses **Apply**. Anything you require (a
membership fee, statutes, a board list) is arranged outside Vote4Dance. Check it before you press
**Approve organization**.

Also check that the club is not a duplicate. Two profiles for one club split its dancers, licenses
and results, and fixing that later needs [Club transfers](/federation/club-transfers/).

## After you approve

1. The club shows **approved** on the Organizations tab, and **issuer** if you allowed license
   issuing.
2. Apply for one license through the club, or ask the club to, and watch it reach your queue. See
   [Setting up licenses](/federation/getting-a-license/).

## What you decide per license item

Whether a license goes through a club is set on each **license item**, not per club:

| Organization mode | Means |
|---|---|
| `Not allowed` | No club on the license. Dancers apply themselves, or you issue it. |
| `Optional` | Either route. |
| `Required` | The license must name a club, and the club must be an approved issuer. |

You also decide whether members may apply for an item themselves, and whether clubs must approve
their members' applications first. See [Setting up licenses](/federation/getting-a-license/).

## What a club does

With the right club roles, an approved club:

- adds its members and approves those who apply to join
- applies for licenses for its members, one at a time, and approves what members apply for
  themselves
- pays for the licenses the club pays for
- creates and registers its competitors for events
- confirms its dancers at check-in on competition day

The club's own guide is under [School/Club](/school-club/).

## Which club a dancer represents

Representation comes from **licenses**, not club membership. A competitor represents the club that
holds the most active dancer licenses among its dancers. So when a dancer moves club, you reissue
the license to the new club. See [Club transfers](/federation/club-transfers/).

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| A club's dancers cannot get licenses, or are all stuck on **Pending** | The club is still **pending**. Approve it, then the licenses can be approved. |
| The club is approved but cannot apply for licenses that need a club | **Allow license issuing** is not on, or the item's organization mode is `Not allowed`. |
| A club says it cannot apply to you | A club belongs to one federation at a time. It must detach from its current one first. |
| Approving fails with a permission error | Your role is Viewer. Approving needs Manager or higher. |
| Two profiles exist for one club | Pick one. Moving licenses and teams to it is done from [Club transfers](/federation/club-transfers/). |
