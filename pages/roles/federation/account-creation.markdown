# Federation staff accounts

Everyone who works for the federation in Vote4Dance uses their **own personal account**, with a
role in the federation. There are no shared federation logins.

## Step 1: Each person creates a personal account

Everyone creates their own account at vote4dance.com, with their own email address. See
[Account creation](/dancer/account-creation/): the form is the same for everybody.

## Step 2: Add them to the federation

`Federation → Settings → Roles & access`. Search for the person by name or email, and press
**Add**. They start as **Viewer**. They must already have an account.

Adding staff needs the **Administrator** or **Owner** role. See
[Federation settings](/federation/settings/#roles--access).

## Step 3: Give each person the right role

| Role | Give it to | Can do |
|---|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Board members, auditors | Read everything, change nothing. |
| **Manager** | The licence secretary | Issue, approve, suspend and revoke licences. Open Competitions and Statistics. |
| **Administrator** | Whoever runs the federation's structure | Everything a Manager does, plus divisions, classes, rules, rankings, class memberships, approving clubs, Stripe, settings. Can add staff up to Manager. |
| **Owner** | The person ultimately responsible | Everything, including making others Administrator or Owner. |

**Approving clubs needs Administrator**, not Manager. A licence secretary who also approves
clubs needs Administrator. See [Federation staff roles](/federation/staff-roles/).

## Step 4: Make sure you are not the only one

- Have **at least two Owners**. Nobody can change or remove their own role, and there is no
  one-step ownership transfer.
- Have at least one other person who can approve licences, for holidays and the season rush.

## Check it works

Each person signs in with their own account, opens the federation, and checks they see what their
role should see. Viewers see no menu items; they open federation pages by link.

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| **Add** fails | The person has no Vote4Dance account with that email, or is already on the list. |
| Someone can see a button but the save fails | Their role is too low. Managers see some Administrator buttons. |
| You cannot change someone's role | An Administrator can only change Viewers and Managers. Ask an Owner. |
