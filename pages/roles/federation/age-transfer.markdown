# Age moves: your part

Your age rules are applied in two places:

| When | What happens | Who |
|---|---|---|
| **At every registration** | Vote4Dance checks the competitor against the class's age group and your age category rules. | Automatic |
| **At the start of each season** | The **age-up batch** moves competitors that have outgrown their age group to the same class one age group up. | You run it, `Federation → Members → Competitors` |

## Before the season: set the rules

1. **Season start (MM-DD)**, so ages are counted for the right year. Age is counted by birth
   year: the year the season ends minus the birth year.
2. **Age groups** with their bands, under `Federation → Structure`. See
   [Divisions](/federation/divisions/#age-groups).
3. **Whose age counts** for couples and groups: for example the oldest member, or every member.
   See [Age category rules](/federation-rules/age-category-rules/).
4. **Ranking carry on age move**, if results should follow a competitor into the new age group's
   ranking.

Check the combined result in `Federation → Rulebook`. Publish the rules to clubs before the season,
so the batch holds no surprises.

## At the start of the season: run the batch

1. Set the **Effective date**.
2. **Preview**. Every candidate is listed with its current and target class.
3. **Tick** the competitors to move. Nothing is ticked by default. Under most rules a competitor
   may stay in an age group with no upper limit, so tick only those that must move or have started
   in the new one.
4. Optionally set a points **carryover**.
5. **Run age-up**.

Rows that cannot move are hidden at first, and each keeps its reason. The full procedure:
[Age moves](/federation/age-moves/). Running the batch needs the **Administrator** role.

## Edge cases

| Case | What to do |
|---|---|
| A couple's younger partner is below the target band | Decide it in your rules, not case by case. **Oldest member decides** on the target class or category makes such couples movable, and registration then accepts them too. |
| A competitor needs a move outside the batch | Edit the class membership on the Competitors tab. It is recorded in the history. |
| A dancer has no birth date | They add it to their account. Without it, some age groups block registration. |
