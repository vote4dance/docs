# School or club renew license cycles

Renewal cycles are your season-transition control point. Done early, they prevent registration delays and eligibility gaps.

## There is no renew button

A licence is not renewed in place — **you apply again for the new season**. The old one stays in
history and turns **Expired** when its validity window closes; the new one is a fresh application
that goes through the same route and the same four checks as the first.

The product says as much where it matters: a dancer blocked at registration is told "A required
federation license has expired or is for a different season — renew it for this season."

Two consequences worth planning around:

- **Nothing carries over.** Approval from last season does not shorten this season's approval, and
  a club approval or a payment is needed again.
- **The season year is the thing to get right.** A licence for the wrong season is as useless as an
  expired one, and it is the most common mistake in a rollover.

## When to start

Work backwards from the first registration deadline of the new season, not from a fixed number of
days: each application needs the club's approval, a payment, and then a person at the federation.
Check that the licences you need are **Active**, not only submitted, before registration opens.

## How to do it

1. **Make a list** of the members who will compete next season, and which licence item each
   needs.
2. **Check with the federation** whether its licence items, prices or rules have changed for the
   new season.
3. **Apply for each member**, one at a time, on `Organization → Administration → Licenses`: pick
   the member, the item and the **new season year**. There is no bulk apply. Members who apply
   themselves do it on their own `Account → Licenses`, and you approve them under
   **Needs your action**.
4. **Pay** the ones the club pays for. **Pay** is enabled once the licence is approved.
5. **Follow them to Active.** A licence can wait on the club's approval, the club's own federation
   approval, the payment, or the validity window. Whatever is missing is named on the licence.

A licence is identified by the member, the item and the season; there are no application numbers.
Keep your own list of who is still waiting.

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| Applied for the wrong season | A pending application can be removed: the delete button on its row. Then apply again. |
| Paid, but still Pending | Payment and approval are separate. The federation still has to approve it. Do not apply again. |
| A member cannot be selected | They have no birth date on their account, or are no longer a member of the club. |
| Started too late | Apply first for members with the nearest competitions, and tell the others when to expect it. |
