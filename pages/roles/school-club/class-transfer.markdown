# Class and age moves

A couple or team changes class in two ways. It moves up a class level when it has earned enough
promotion points, and it moves to the next age group when a new season starts. The club does not
run or approve either move. Your part is to make sure your dancers' details are right, to register
each competitor in the class it is in now, and to look over your entries before registration
closes.

## What moves automatically

### Class moves

Class moves are **automatic**. Every competition entry earns promotion points. When the organizer
closes a competition, the points are added up, and a couple that reaches its class's limit moves
to the next class at once. The federation can also move a couple by hand.

How it works, in more detail: [Moving up a class](/dancer/class-transfer/) and
[Promotion points](/federation/progression/).

### Age moves

At the start of each season, the **federation** moves competitors that have outgrown their age
group into the same class one age group up.

- Age is counted by **birth year**, for the year the season ends, not by birthday.
- For a couple or team, the **federation's rules** decide whose age counts. Often the older
  partner decides, but some age groups need every dancer to have reached the age.
- An age group with an upper limit must be left. From an age group with no upper limit, a couple
  can usually stay until it starts competing in the next one.
- Registration checks all of this. A class a competitor does not fit is shown as locked, with the
  reason.

How the move works for a dancer: [Moving to a new age group](/dancer/age-transfer/). The
federation's side, including what blocks a move: [Age moves](/federation/age-moves/).

## What the club can do

### Before the season

1. Make sure every dancer has a **birth date** on their account. Dancers add it themselves under
   **My account** → **Settings**. Without it, you also cannot apply for their license.
2. Tell couples close to an age limit which age group they will be in, and to ask the federation
   if they are unsure.

### Before you register

- **Check classes before registering.** After a competition closes, a couple may have moved up.
  Registering in the class it has just left is the most common mistake.
- **Remember points belong to the couple or team.** A new partnership, or a group with a new
  lineup, can be a new competitor with no points. Where the federation requires class membership,
  a new competitor can register for entry-level classes and is placed there when it registers. For
  a higher class, the federation has to place it there first. A competitor that already holds a
  higher class can't register for the entry-level class of the same ladder.

### Before registration closes

1. Look over your club's entries on `Organization → Administration → Registration`.
2. An entry in the wrong class or the wrong age group: move it to the right class with
   **Change class** while the period is open, see
   [Moving an entry to another class](/school-club/registration-for-event/#moving-an-entry-to-another-class).
   If the right class is not offered there, cancel the entry and register again. After the period
   closes, [ask the organizer](/getting-help/#reaching-the-organizer).

## If the federation has a couple in the wrong class

The club cannot change a competitor's class or points. Ask the federation, and include the couple,
the competitions it danced, and the class you expect.

| What you see | Most likely reason |
|---|---|
| A couple should have moved up, but has not | The last competition is not closed yet. |
| A couple's points are split over two couples | A wrong federation ID on an entry created a duplicate couple. The organizer sees this number as **Person ID**. |
| The club and the federation disagree about a class | Go by the federation's answer before registering. |

### The wrong age group

The club cannot change a competitor's age group either. Ask the federation, and include the
couple, the dancers' birth years, the age group they are in and the one you expect.
