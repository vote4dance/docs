# How members join

Dancers and staff join your club themselves. Your part is to make the club easy to find, and to approve the people who apply.

## The mechanics on your side

**Member approval** is a setting in the club's details: "New users need to be approved before
becoming members". With it on, the people who ask to join wait under **Members** as **Requires
your action**, and you approve them one by one or with **Approve all**. With it off, anybody who
asks is a member at once. You change it with **Edit organization** on the club's **Home** page.

Approving takes at least the **Manager** role. Everyone joins as a **Member**. That is the right
role for a dancer. Give **Coach**, **Manager** or **Administrator** only to the people who do
that work: what each role can do is listed under [Roles](/school-club/club-admin/#roles).

## How members find and join the club

1. The dancer creates their own personal account. See [Creating your account](/dancer/account-creation/).
2. They find the club and press **Join organization**.
3. With **Member approval** on, they wait under **Members** until a manager or administrator
   approves them. With it off, they are members at once.

The club cannot add people itself: everyone joins by pressing **Join organization**. That way each
member has agreed that the club and its federation can see their details and manage their
registrations. Coaches and other staff join the same way; an administrator then gives them their
role under **Permissions**.

## Make it easy to find the right club

- Use the club's full, official name, and a clear **Short letters** tag. The tag shows in start
  lists and results.
- Fill in the city and country, so members can tell your club from one with a similar name.
- Tell new members the exact club name to look for, and who approves them.

## Before you tell members to join

- At least two people have the **Administrator** role. Only Vote4Dance can add the
  second one, so [ask early](/getting-help/#writing-to-vote4dance).
- Someone with **Manager** or higher will approve applications, if **Member approval** is on.
- The club's details are complete.

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| A member joined the wrong club | They leave it and join yours. Check their license names your club too: an active license's club can only be changed by the federation, by reissuing it. |
| Applications are waiting and nobody approves them | Only managers and administrators can approve. Coaches do not see the waiting list. There is no reminder. |
| There are two profiles for your club | Do not add members to both. Ask your federation which one is the real club; moving competitors and licenses between clubs is done by the federation. See [Club transfers](/federation/club-transfers/). |
| You cannot find a way to add someone | Nobody is added from the club's side. Send them the club's page and ask them to press **Join organization**. |
