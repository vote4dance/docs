# Applying for a school or club

At organization level, this step means making your school/club profile clear, searchable, and ready for members to join without confusion.

## The mechanics on your side

**Member approval** is a switch on the club: "New users need to be approved before becoming
members". With it on, applications queue on the members list as **Requires your action**, and you
approve them one by one or with **Approve all**. With it off, anybody who applies is in.

Approving takes at least the **Manager** role. What you give people afterwards matters more than
the approval itself:

| Role | Give it to |
|---|---|
| **Member** | Dancers. They represent the club and coaches can enter them; they cannot register on the club's behalf or manage members. |
| **Coach** | Whoever creates competitors, registers them and pays for the club's registrations. |
| **Manager** | Whoever approves memberships and handles the club's federation licences. |
| **Administrator** | Whoever runs the club: invites people, sets roles below administrator, applies to federations, edits or deletes the club. |

A dancer left on **Member** is correct, not a mistake to fix.

## How members find and join the club

1. The dancer creates their own personal account. See [Account creation](/dancer/account-creation/).
2. They find the club and press **Join organization**.
3. With **Member approval** on, they wait under **Members** until a manager or administrator
   approves them. With it off, they are members at once.

An administrator can also add someone directly: **Permissions → Search members**, pick the
person, **Add**. They must already have a Vote4Dance account. There is no invitation email.

## Make it easy to find the right club

- Use the club's full, official name, and a clear **Short letters** tag. The tag shows in start
  lists and results.
- Fill in the city and country, so members can tell your club from one with a similar name.
- Tell new members the exact club name to look for, and who approves them.

## Before you tell members to join

- At least two people have the **Administrator** role.
- Someone with **Manager** or higher will approve applications, if **Member approval** is on.
- The club's details are complete.

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| A member joined the wrong club | They leave it and join yours. Check their licence names your club too: an active licence's club can only be changed by the federation, by reissuing it. |
| Applications are waiting and nobody approves them | Only managers and administrators can approve. Coaches do not see the waiting list. There is no reminder. |
| There are two profiles for your club | Do not add members to both. Ask your federation which one is the real club; moving competitors and licences between clubs is done by the federation. See [Club transfers](/federation/club-transfers/). |
| **Add** does nothing | Only an administrator can add people, the search needs at least three letters, and the person needs an account. |
