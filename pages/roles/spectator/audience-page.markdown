# The Audience page

Every event has an **Audience** tab on its page on the website. It shows what is going on for the audience and the results so far, and is open to everyone, signed in or not.

## What the page shows

- **Happening now**: the quiz that is on, with the question it is on and how many are playing, and the rounds on the floor that you can give hearts to or call. Each has a button that takes you to the app.
- **All day**: quizzes you can play whenever you like, and until when.
- **Later today**: quizzes that start by themselves later.
- **Results so far**: the **Audience favourite** of every published round, with its hearts, and the results of quizzes that are finished, with a **Standings** link.

A quiz's results have their own page with the winner, the top of the standings and how each question went. You can share the link. The results stay there until the end of the event day. Full standings beyond the top 10 are in the app with Premium.

## Taking part

Taking part happens in the app, under your own name. The web page says **Take part in the Vote4Dance app** and has an **Open in the app** button. See [Quizzes](/spectator/quizzes/) and [Hearts and Call it](/spectator/hearts-and-calls/).

Quizzes and calls never change the judges' results. Hearts only count towards the result where the organizer has seated an **Audience** judge.
