# Scrutineer event assignment

Scrutineers are typically assigned rather than self-registering like dancers. This step covers how event work becomes official.

## What this step is for

This step explains how organizers and scrutineers align on event access and operational responsibilities.

## How you actually get access

There is no scrutineer self-registration. Somebody with Manager on the event adds you:

1. They open `Manager → Event → Users`
2. They find your account with **Search user by name or email** — so you need a personal account
   first, and they need the address it uses
3. They tick the apps you need
4. Optionally they give you a **role title** such as "Chief scrutineer". It is only a label; access
   follows the ticked apps.

**Which apps to ask for:**

| App | Why |
|---|---|
| **Supervisor** | Your own tool: unpublished results, judge marks, offsets, live judging status. Ask for this one always. |
| **Manager** | Only if you also work the desk. It is full access to the setup, which most scrutineers neither need nor want. |
| **Speaker** | Only if you will be the one publishing results from the floor console. |

Ticking Manager does **not** give you Supervisor, or the other way round: the apps are ticked one
by one. If you can open the event but not the app you expect, that is why.

## Before the event

1. **Sign in and open the event.** You should see a Supervisor tile. Do this days before, not on
   the morning.
2. **Check the panels.** Every judge has a type — Judge, Chief judge (in), Chief judge (out),
   Trainee, Audience — and the type decides whether their marks count. Confirm the types are what
   the federation expects, especially which chief judge is *in* and which is *out*.
3. **Check Problems** in Manager with the organizer, or ask them to. Rounds without judges, group
   classes without judges, overlapping scheduling and start-number conflicts are all things you
   would otherwise find during the competition.
4. **Agree who publishes.** You hold the permission; so does the speaker. Decide which of you
   presses it, and what you want to have checked before it happens.
5. **Agree the escalation path** for a correction after a result is published.

## What success looks like

You open the Supervisor app on the day, see the current round's marks as they arrive, and nothing
about your access or the panel setup is a surprise.
