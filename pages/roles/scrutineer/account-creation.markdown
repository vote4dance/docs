# Scrutineer account creation

This is the first step for a scrutineer. It makes sure event access starts from a clean personal account.

## What this step is for

The account identifies the scrutineer for event assignments, permissions, and operational accountability.

## How it works

1. Create a personal account at vote4dance.com: choose **Sign in**, type your email, press
   **Continue** and type the code that is mailed to you. A new address then asks for your first
   name, last name, nationality and the terms checkbox. There is no password to invent, and you are
   signed in straight away. The steps are on [Signing in](/signing-in/), and the form is the same
   one every role uses, described on [Dancer account creation](/dancer/account-creation/).
2. Use the **name the federation and organizers know you by**. You are found by name or email when
   somebody adds you to an event, so a nickname makes you hard to find.
3. Tell the organizers **which email address** your account uses. That is the one they search for.

**One account, never a shared one.** Access is given per event to a person, and the log of who did
what follows the account. A shared "scrutineer@club" login makes an event's history worthless.

## What success looks like

An organizer can find you under `Manager → Event → Users` by name or email, and tick **Supervisor**
for you. See [Event assignment](/scrutineer/registration-for-event/).
