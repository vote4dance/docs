# Class transfers at your event

Class moves are automatic. A couple moves up a class when its promotion points reach the class's
limit, and that happens when a competition it danced in is **closed**. The federation can also
move a couple by hand. You do not approve or run class moves.

For you this means two things:

- **Close your competition** once results are final, or your dancers' points and class moves do
  not happen. See [Results, points and rankings](/organizer/creating-and-getting-a-ranking/).
- **Registration checks the class for you**, from each class's Federation Class. If a dancer
  says they are in the wrong class, see
  [When a dancer says they are in the wrong class](/organizer/age-transfer/#when-a-dancer-says-they-are-in-the-wrong-class).

Age moves, and what to check yourself, are on [Age and class transfers](/organizer/age-transfer/).
The full points mechanism is on [Promotion points](/federation/progression/).
