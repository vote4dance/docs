# Running an event under a federation

You do not apply for anything in Vote4Dance to run an event under a federation. **You choose the
federation for the event**, and its classes, licence checks and rules then apply.

Your federation may still have its own requirements outside Vote4Dance, such as sanctioning the
date or expecting your club to be a member. Ask them before you announce the event.

## How to choose the federation

When you create the event, the first question is **How do you want to start?**

| Choice | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Training competition with default settings** | No federation. No federation classes or licence checks. Good for a first event or a test. |
| **Competition for a specific federation** | You pick the federation. Its rules, classes and licence checks apply, and the event appears on the federation's Competitions list. |

You can change the federation later on the event's details page, and the event moves to the new
federation's list at once. **Do it before you build classes and open registration**: classes map
to the federation's classes, so changing federation afterwards means redoing them.

See [Event setup](/manager-guide/event-setup/).

## What the federation decides for your event

| What | How it reaches your event |
|---|---|
| **Age and class rules** | Each of your classes maps to a **Federation Class**, which carries its age and level rules. You do not enter ages yourself. |
| **Licence requirements** | Checked automatically when dancers register. See [Licences](/organizer/getting-a-license/). |
| **Registration rules** | For example who may register a competitor, whether custom team names are allowed, and dance-role requirements. |
| **Promotion points and rankings** | Processed when you **close** the competition. See [Results, points and rankings](/organizer/creating-and-getting-a-ranking/). |

## Before you open registration

1. The event has the right federation.
2. Every class maps to the right **Federation Class**. See
   [Competition setup](/manager-guide/competition-setup/#step-1-create-classes).
3. [Problems](/manager-guide/validator/) in Manager shows nothing you have not dealt with.
4. You have asked the federation whether it needs anything else from you or your club.

## Common problems

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| The event was created as a training competition, but it should count for a federation | Change the federation on the event's details page, then check every class's Federation Class. |
| A class dancers expect is missing | The federation may not have that Federation Class, or it is inactive. Ask the federation. |
| Dancers are blocked by a rule you did not expect | The rule comes from the federation. Check the class's Federation Class, and ask the federation if it is wrong. |
