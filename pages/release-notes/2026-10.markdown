# Release notes, October 2026

The newest changes are on [Release notes](/release-notes/).

## 2026-10-30
 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Federation: **Licenses** → **All other licenses** has a **Filter by organization**, so you can see which dancers of one club or school hold a license; type part of the club's name to find it. **Download CSV** follows the filter, and the search box also finds a club by name (see [Licenses of one organization](/federation-licenses/federation-admin/#licenses-of-one-organization))
* Public: The invoice and receipt a club gets when it pays for registrations now has one line per team or dancer that names the team, its dancers and its classes, for example "Winter Cup – Main: Sparkles (Anna Andersson, Erik Berg) — Disco Duo Juniors". Before, every line only said the event and the registration period. Invoices sent before this change stay as they are (see [Step 4: Follow up and pay](/school-club/registration-for-event/#following-up-and-paying))
* App: Start numbers in a round's heats stay on one line and are easier to read, also when the phone's text size is turned up; before, a four-digit number could break over two lines (see [Find results](/dancer/following-in-the-app/#find-results))
* Public: On a club's **Registration** page, cancelling an entry now asks **Withdraw from** the class by name, says that everyone on the entry gets an email, and, if the entry is paid, that the payment is not refunded automatically: contact the organizer. **Pay now** there has the same line as on **My registration**: your registration is final once paid (see [Cancelling](/school-club/registration-for-event/#cancelling))
* Public: On a club's **Registration** page, **Download list (CSV)** now sits on the selected event and downloads that event's active entries, in a file named after the event; before, **Export CSV** downloaded every entry of all events shown in one file (see [Registration](/school-club/club-admin/#registration))
* Manager: **Statistics** (the overview, **Dressing rooms** and the printed dressing-room lists) now counts every member of a registered group or formation once the participants are imported; before, an entry could still count at most three dancers, so an event with groups showed far too few **Dancers** (see [What Dancers and Organizations count](/manager-guide/statistics/#what-dancers-and-organizations-count))
* Manager: **Statistics** → **Dressing rooms**, its **Print view** and the printed dancer lists now name each club in full, with its short code in brackets, for example **Dansstudio Shake Vzw (SHAKE)**; before, clubs from the imported participants were listed by their short code only (see [The numbers on the page](/manager-guide/statistics/#the-numbers-on-the-page))
* Manager: The **Registrations** list shows the dancers who dance the entry, with lineup changes applied, instead of the team's roster (see [Team names](/manager-guide/registration-periods/#team-names))
* Public, Federation, Manager: A club's and the federation's registration overview, the amount a club pays by invoice and the organizer's club list (CSV) now follow each entry's lineup: dancers skipped for a class are not listed or charged for it, and a replacement is. Before, they still showed the team's whole roster, and a team that skipped different dancers in two classes could be charged for a dancer who sits one out (see [Step 3: Register](/school-club/registration-for-event/#fitting-a-teams-lineup-to-a-class))

## 2026-10-29
 ⭐ **New**
* Federation: The **Class rules** tab has a **Download CSV** button. It saves every rule of the discipline as a spreadsheet row, with where it applies, its condition and each setting in its own column, so you can go through the rules or agree changes outside Vote4Dance (see [Download the rules](/federation-rules/round-setup-rules/#download-the-rules))

 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Speaker: On **Lineup**, every heat card always shows its heat number and how many are dancing in it. When the cards are narrow, for example with two rounds side by side or a zoomed-in browser, the **Done** button shrinks to a ✓ instead of covering the count (see [Live operations](/manager-guide/live-operations/))
* App: The notification you get when a dancer you follow finishes a round now includes their placing whenever the app shows it. Before, the placing was often left out of the notification in rounds where the organizer had not set a position to hide results from (see [Follow dancers and clubs with Premium](/dancer/following-in-the-app/#follow-dancers-and-clubs-with-premium))

## 2026-10-28
 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Manager: Importing registrations now puts a group or formation team on the start list under its team name, also when the name was given to the team and not typed on the registration; before, such a team showed up under its first three dancers on the start list and the public participants list, while the **Registrations** list showed its name. A team name containing **&** now stays whole. To fix a start list you imported earlier, import the registrations again (see [Team names](/manager-guide/registration-periods/#team-names))

## 2026-10-26
 ⭐ **New**
* Manager: A dancer who belongs to more than one club now gets a list under **Represents** in **Edit registration**, so you can correct the club a dancer or parent picked by mistake when registering. Only clubs that have approved the dancer are listed, and clearing the list goes back to the club Vote4Dance finds on its own (see [Change the club a dancer represents](/manager-guide/entries-and-participants/#change-the-club-a-dancer-represents))
* All web apps: You can sign in with just your e-mail. Press **Continue**, type the 6-digit code that Vote4Dance mails you (it works for 10 minutes), and you are in; there is no password to remember. A new e-mail address gets a short form after the code (first name, last name, nationality and the terms), with no password to invent. The button on the code screen says **Continue**, and if you press **Send again** the code in the first e-mail keeps working for 10 minutes, so a late e-mail is not wasted. An account that has a password can use it with **Have a password? Sign in with it** under **Continue** on the first screen, or **This account has a password · use it instead** on the code screen, and **Forgot your password?** still works. On a phone the first screen is shorter: it leaves out **Use a passkey from your phone**. The Vote4Dance mobile app signs in the same way: **Sign in** and **Join Vote4Dance** both ask for your e-mail and the code, the code is also in the e-mail's subject so the notification shows it, and **Use a passkey** and **Have a password? Sign in with it** are on the sign-in screens (see [Sign in with a code](/signing-in/#sign-in-with-a-code))
* All web apps: After you sign in with a code or a password, you are offered once on each device to save a **passkey**, so your face, fingerprint or phone code signs you in next time. On a borrowed computer, choose **Use a passkey from your phone** and scan the code on the screen with your phone. The offer says to save a passkey only on a device that is yours, and **Not now** is now an ordinary button next to the save button. A code to your e-mail always works if you lose your phone (see [Save a passkey](/signing-in/#save-a-passkey) and [Sign in with a passkey](/signing-in/#sign-in-with-a-passkey))
* All web apps: A parent can save a passkey for each of their children's accounts on the parent's phone; the phone lists the accounts by the child's name (see [Children's accounts on a parent's phone](/signing-in/#childrens-accounts-on-a-parents-phone))
* All web apps: Changing or resetting your password now signs your account out on your other devices and removes your saved passkeys, so nobody who knew the old password can stay signed in (see [Use your password](/signing-in/#use-your-password))
* App: After you sign in, the app offers once to save a passkey for the phone, and **Profile → Settings → How you sign in** lists your passkeys, adds one for this phone and shows the e-mail code as always on (see [Save a passkey](/signing-in/#save-a-passkey))
* Public: **My account** → **Settings** has a new **How you sign in** box. It lists your passkeys, where you can remove one or add one on the device you are using, shows the e-mail code as always on, and keeps **Set password** (see [Manage how you sign in](/signing-in/#manage-how-you-sign-in))
* Check-in: An account that the desk creates for a walk-in no longer gets a password. The person signs in with their e-mail address and a code (see [Create an account](/signing-in/#create-an-account))

 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Manager: The **Organization** column on the **Registrations** tab now shows every dancer's club as soon as the page opens; before, a dancer whose club came from their own account showed only after you opened the row (see [Which club a dancer represents](/manager-guide/entries-and-participants/#which-club-a-dancer-represents))
* Registration, Check-in: Dancers you skip from a team's lineup for a class no longer get that class's confirmation e-mail or check-in QR code, are no longer let in at the check-in desk as dancing in it, and no longer show for it in the club's and organizer's registration lists or on **My event**. A replacement dancer gets all of these instead. Before, everyone on the team got them, even though only the dancers in the lineup were charged (see [Step 3: Register](/school-club/registration-for-event/#fitting-a-teams-lineup-to-a-class))
* Manager: **Statistics → Dressing rooms**, **Day by day** and **Print dancer lists** now count and list every dancer of a group or formation, following the line-up the club sent for each class. Before, once the participants were imported, an entry counted at most three dancers, so events with groups and formations showed far too few **Dancers** and the dancer lists missed people. A dancer who is both in a registered entry and in a couple put together on the start list (a Jack & Jill final) now counts once. The tab now also says above the numbers what **Dancers** and **Organizations** count (see [What Dancers and Organizations count](/manager-guide/statistics/#what-dancers-and-organizations-count))

## 2026-10-25
 🛠️ **Improvements**
* App: Tapping **Vote** in a round without Premium now closes the round and shows the Premium offer; before, nothing seemed to happen on iPhone and the app stopped responding to taps once the round was closed (see [Vote for a competitor](/dancer/following-in-the-app/#vote-for-a-competitor))
* Manager: The **Audience** judge now shows as handed in once the last of the other judges hands in, and can be unlocked like any other judge, for example after re-opening a round so later votes count again; before, it always looked open and could not be unlocked (see [Judge types](/manager-guide/judges/#judge-types))
* Manager: **Schedule conflicts** and **Problems** now flag a dancer entered in two classes that start at the same time on one floor in the same schedule item, since those classes are danced together; before, two rounds on the same floor were never a conflict (see [What counts as a conflict](/manager-guide/schedule/#what-counts-as-a-conflict))
* Manager: In an event with several competitions, **Problems** lists dancers in overlapping rounds and judges on two floors once, under **Event** at the top, instead of once under every competition (see [Reading the page](/manager-guide/validator/#reading-the-page))
* Manager, Screens: A judge is shown with the **Photo** uploaded on the **Judges** page, or a placeholder when there is none. The judge's Vote4Dance ID photo, the one the check-in desk compares faces with, is no longer used as a stand-in on screens or in judge lists (see [Judge list](/manager-guide/judges/#judge-list))
* Manager: **Schedule conflicts** now lists a dancer who goes from one class straight into another on the same floor as **Tight**; before, two rounds on the same floor were never compared, so an event on one floor showed no conflicts at all. **Hide tight transitions within the same hall** only appears when the event has rounds on a floor's sections, and only hides moves between the whole floor and its sections (see [What counts as a conflict](/manager-guide/schedule/#what-counts-as-a-conflict))
* App: On Android, the icons in the tab bar and on the event pages show again after updating the app. In version 1.13.3 they could stay blank if you updated from an earlier version; update to 1.13.4 (see [Following in the app](/dancer/following-in-the-app/))

## 2026-10-24
 ⭐ **New**
* Public: **My registration** has a **Change class** button. Until registration closes you can move your entry to another class that opens for registration together with yours, for example another level or region, without withdrawing and registering again. The price is the same, so nothing is charged or refunded; a duo or team moves together, and a class you are not allowed to dance is shown greyed out with the reason (see [Changing class](/dancer/my-registration/#changing-class))

 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Manager: On the **Judges** page, a **Chief judge (in)** now counts as one of the panel's judges. A panel that wants 5 judges with a **Chief judge (in)** is full with 4 judges plus the chief, so it no longer shows "Still needed: 1 × Judge" or a warning on the **Validator**; before, the chief was counted separately. A **Chief judge (out)** still does not count towards the judges, and a panel with a **Chief judge (in)** on its **Seats wanted** now needs one who marks, not a **Chief judge (out)** (see [A panel's settings](/manager-guide/judge-panels/#a-panels-settings))
* Public: Opening an event's **Registration** tab without being signed in now shows how registration works and a **Sign in** button that brings you back to the same page, instead of sending you straight to the sign-in page (see [Registering on your own (solo)](/dancer/registration-for-event/#registering-on-your-own-solo))
* Public: **Pay now** has a line under it saying that your registration is final once paid, and if you have paid, confirming a **Withdraw** reminds you that the payment is not refunded automatically: contact the organizer. The card for a class with no start time yet now says **Your dance time is announced after registration closes** (see [Withdrawing from a class](/dancer/my-registration/#withdrawing-from-a-class))

## 2026-10-22
 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Manager, Registration: Solo and couple entries, and entries in classes without a federation class, now appear under the dancers' names on the **Registrations** list, in the cart, on start lists, in registration e-mails, on **My event**, in the club's registration lists and at the check-in desk, instead of a nickname given to the team. Group and formation classes keep their team names. To collect team names in another class, add **Participant name** under **Requested information** in its **Registration rules**. A name you type on a registration still shows and is imported (see [Team names](/manager-guide/registration-periods/#team-names))

## 2026-10-19
 ⭐ **New**
* Manager: **Print dancer lists** on **Statistics** and **Check-in** prints every club's dancers with their classes, start numbers and a box to tick on arrival, for the entrance when the network is down or to hand to the clubs. Pick a day to list only that day's dancers, and tick **One page per organization** for a page per club (see [Dancer lists](/manager-guide/statistics/#dancer-lists))
* Manager: **Download dancers (CSV)** and **Download organizations (CSV)** on the dancer lists give every dancer's date of birth and what each dancer and club has paid and still owes, so clubs can ask their dancers to pay back the fees (see [Downloading the lists](/manager-guide/statistics/#downloading-the-lists))
* Public: Clubs can see their dancers for each day of an event. The new **By day** view under **Administration → Registration** lists each dancer once per day with the classes they dance that day, and **Export CSV** downloads the list. This helps a club that pays a per-day fee for its dancers to collect it from them. The days come from the event's schedule, so the lists appear once the organizer has published it (see [Dancers per day](/school-club/club-admin/#dancers-per-day))

 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Federation: The class rule editor can add a round before the first round, such as an unjudged presentation or warm-up: set **Which rounds** to **Before the first round** and the rule adds that round to the classes and sizes it is for. **Rounds this hits** and **Example** show the added round first, and rules that already add such a round open with the choice picked (see [A round before the first round](/federation-rules/round-setup-rules/#a-round-before-the-first-round))
* Manager: A class whose short letter is one of the federation's class codes is now linked to that Federation Class when you create, rename or split it, so the federation's age, level and round rules reach it without picking the class by hand; before, only classes created by importing a file were linked (see [Step 1: Create classes](/manager-guide/competition-setup/#step-1-create-classes))
* Manager: The diploma guide has a downloadable A4 template and sample printout for designing your own pre-printed diploma paper: they show where each line is printed and which corner to keep free for the Vote4Dance mark (see [Designing your own diploma paper](/manager-guide/diploma-customization/#designing-your-own-diploma-paper))
* App: If you bought Premium on the Vote4Dance website, **Cancel subscription** under **Profile → Settings** now opens your subscription in your browser, where you can change or cancel it; before, it only pointed to the App Store or Play Store, which do not list subscriptions bought on the website. Premium bought in the app is still cancelled in the App Store or Play Store (see [Cancel Premium](/dancer/following-in-the-app/#cancel-premium))

 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Federation: Class rule **Findings** now report rules for a category, level or age group the division no longer has (**Deleted value**) and rules whose settings cannot be read (**Cannot be read**), which are skipped when rounds are made. Both have a **Switch off** button that keeps the rule saved but stops it applying; you can switch it on again in the rule editor. A rule whose settings are not valid can no longer be saved (see [Findings](/federation-rules/round-setup-checks/#findings))
* Manager: The printed schedule now shows the time each round is planned to start, also with **Group by classes** on; a new **Show round times** option leaves them out (see [Printing](/manager-guide/schedule/#printing))
* Public: In the lists of events, an event that runs over the end of a month is now listed under the month it starts in, the same date its card shows; before, an event from 28 October to 1 November was listed under November

## 2026-10-17
 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Public: A published event's page now has an address made from its name, such as vote4dance.com/public/event/snow-ball-2026, instead of a number. Links, QR codes and e-mails with the old numbered address keep working and open the same page. E-mails, payment pages and the app now link to the readable address, and a shared link shows the event's name and picture either way. While an event is **Hidden** its page keeps the numbered address; the readable one follows the event's name and date until you publish, and renaming a published event does not change it (see [Step 2: Event status](/manager-guide/event-setup/#step-2-event-status))

 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Manager, Supervisor: Under each judge's name on the **Panels** tab of **Judges**, the rounds and minutes now count only this competition; the "(N in other competitions)" note is gone. Seats in the event's other competitions still count when checking overlaps, rounds per day and breaks (see [Panels](/manager-guide/judge-panels/#panels))
* Event page: Helpers with **Check-in** or **Café & sales** access now see their **Registration** or **Café & sales** tile before the event opens, so they can find their way in before the day (see [Getting a helper in before the day](/manager-guide/users-and-stations/#getting-a-helper-in-before-the-day))
* Check-in: On a phone the **Scan** button is an icon, so the search box keeps room for its hint, and the arrived tag and country flag stay on the same line as the person's name (see [Check-in on a phone](/manager-guide/the-door/#check-in-on-a-phone))
* Check-in: The cookie notice no longer hides the last card on a person's page; you can scroll it into view above the notice

## 2026-10-15
 ⭐ **New**
* Scrutineer (Beta): **Add earlier judges** on the **Judges** page lists the judges of your earlier events and, once you have run an event in the federation before, of its other events, so you tick them instead of searching for each one; one click picks the whole panel of a recent event (see [Judges from earlier events](/manager-guide/judges/#judges-from-earlier-events))

 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Federation: **Statistics** follow your federation's season instead of the calendar year. With a season starting 1 September, "this season" runs from 1 September to 31 August, and **Entries per season** is labelled by season, such as **2025/2026** (see [The season](/federation/statistics/#the-season))
* Federation: Licences with no season, such as those checked against your federation's own member register, now count on **Statistics**; before, the licence figures stayed at 0 when all your licences were of that kind (see [The season](/federation/statistics/#the-season))
* Federation: **Classes that did not fill** on **Statistics** only counts classes at competitions that have already been danced, out of the classes danced so far; before, every class at a competition still to come counted as not filled (see [The calendar](/federation/statistics/#the-calendar))
* Federation: A **Season start (MM-DD)** that is not a real date, such as 02-30 or 9-01, is no longer saved; a message says how to write it (see [Before the season: set the rules](/federation/age-moves/#season-start))
* Public: On a phone held upright, the schedule shows the time each round is planned to start where the block number used to be, so you no longer need to turn the phone sideways to see it (see [Times on your phone](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#times-on-your-phone))
* Public and App: In the schedule, finished blocks and the notes above the block that is dancing now are grey, so the current block stands out; the green dot stays (see [Finished blocks turn grey](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#finished-blocks-turn-grey))
* Public and App: Once a day is under way, finished blocks above the two most recent ones fold away; **Show earlier blocks** at the top brings them back, and the choice is remembered for the event. Blocks still waiting for results never fold away (see [Earlier blocks fold away](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#earlier-blocks-fold-away))
* Public: The schedule has a **How the schedule works** link to the new [Following the schedule](/spectator/following-the-schedule/) guide
* Manager, Supervisor: Searching for a judge to add waits until you stop typing, so a few quick searches no longer stop with "You have searched for people many times in a short while." (see [An existing account](/manager-guide/judges/#an-existing-account))
* Manager, Supervisor: On the **Panels** tab of **Judges**, a panel with more judges or trainee judges than it wants, such as "2/1", now shows its count in red, just like a panel that is short; hover over a red count to see what is wrong, such as "More than wanted: 1 × Judge" (see [Panels](/manager-guide/judge-panels/#panels))
* Manager, Supervisor: A panel added with **New panel** on the **Panels** tab of **Judges** keeps the number of judges it was created with; before, a panel whose **Seats wanted** you never changed could switch, for example from 5 to 3, once most of the other panels had 3 judges (see [A panel's settings](/manager-guide/judge-panels/#a-panels-settings))
* Federation: A finding that covers many rules or classes can now be marked as intended; before, **Mark as intended** failed for such a finding and nothing was saved (see [Findings](/federation-rules/round-setup-checks/#findings))
* Federation: Searching for a person under **User** when you add or edit a licence now waits until you stop typing, and a search that cannot run shows why instead of an empty list. Federation Managers, who add and edit licences, can now search for the person too, and federation staff can search far more often before being asked to wait a minute (see [Add a license manually](/federation-licenses/federation-admin/#add-a-license-manually))
* Federation: When you add or edit a licence, **Save** asks you to pick a person from the list under **User**; before, a name you typed without picking it was sent in place of the person (see [Add a license manually](/federation-licenses/federation-admin/#add-a-license-manually))
* Federation: **Roles & access** shows the staff list and the search box for adding staff to Administrators and Owners, who manage staff; Viewers and Managers see a note saying so instead of an empty list (see [Invitation flow](/federation/staff-roles/#invitation-flow))

## 2026-10-12
 ⭐ **New**
* App: **Tap to Pay on iPhone** — desk staff take contactless cards, Apple Pay and other digital wallets right on their iPhone, with no card reader, in version 1.13.0 of the app; turn it on once from the event's **Tap to Pay on iPhone** card (see [Tap to Pay on iPhone](/manager-guide/tap-to-pay/))
* App: **Sell** from the phone — add products, then pay with **Tap to Pay on iPhone** or **Cash**; the result shows **Paid**, **Declined** or **Timed out**, with **Try another card** after a decline (see [Selling from the phone](/manager-guide/tap-to-pay/#selling-from-the-phone))
* App: A card payment that is declined e-mails the customer a receipt saying nothing was charged, and notifies the staff member who started the sale (see [Receipts](/manager-guide/tap-to-pay/#receipts))
* App: On Android the same feature is called **Card payments on this phone**; it needs Android 13 or later with developer options turned off, and an older phone offers cash sales only (see [On an Android phone](/manager-guide/tap-to-pay/#on-an-android-phone))
* Check-in: **Take a card** on the till hands the sale to a staff member's phone by QR code (see [Taking a card from the web till](/manager-guide/tap-to-pay/#taking-a-card-from-the-web-till))
* Federation: The class rules of a discipline are shown in groups: rules that do exactly the same thing are one row, with where it applies and how many classes it reaches, such as "1 saved rule · reaches 15 of 156 classes". **Flat list** shows every rule as before (see [The rules list](/federation-rules/round-setup-rules/#the-rules-list))
* Federation: New **Findings** tab on the class rules, and a **Class rule findings** page under **Structure** that checks every discipline. They report copies that differ, gaps in the round sizes, rules no class reaches, rules that never take effect and round conditions that do the opposite of what they say; you can answer a finding or mark it as intended with a reason (see [Findings](/federation-rules/round-setup-checks/#findings) and [Across the federation](/federation-rules/round-setup-checks/#across-the-federation))
* Federation: Reviewing a finding shows the rules involved, who it touched and what was intended, with a recommended answer and a before and after per class (see [Reviewing a finding](/federation-rules/round-setup-checks/#reviewing-a-finding))
* Federation: New **By class** tab on the class rules: pick a class and a number of competitors to see every rule in the order it is read, which applied, which did not fire and why, the rounds the manager will create, and the classes that fall back to the built-in ladder (see [Check a class](/federation-rules/round-setup-checks/#check-a-class))
