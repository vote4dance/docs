# Which app is which

Vote4Dance is one account and several apps. Dancers, parents and the audience use the website
and the Vote4Dance app. Everyone who works at a competition is given one or more of the working
apps by the organizer.

## For dancers, clubs and the audience

| Where | What you do there | Read more |
|---|---|---|
| **The website**, vote4dance.com | Find events, register, pay, manage your club, buy tickets, see results and rankings | [Dancer](/dancer/), [School/Club](/school-club/), [Spectator](/spectator/) |
| **The Vote4Dance app** on your phone | Follow dancers and clubs, see the schedule and live results, show your Vote4Dance ID at the entrance, vote and play the quiz | [Following results in the app](/dancer/following-in-the-app/), [Your Vote4Dance ID](/dancer/vote4dance-id/) |

## For the people working at a competition

The organizer gives each person exactly the apps they need. How that is done, and the shared
devices called stations, is on [Users, apps and stations](/manager-guide/users-and-stations/).

| App | Who uses it | What it is for | Read more |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Manager** | The organizer and the scrutineering desk | Setting up the event and its competitions, registration, participants, judges, running the rounds, results and publishing. Labelled "Scrutineer" in the list of users. | [Organizer](/organizer/) |
| **Judging** | The judges | Marking the rounds. Each judge sets their own PIN on their own device. | [Judges](/manager-guide/judges/) |
| **Supervisor** | Chief scrutineer, chairperson of adjudicators, federation observer | Looking at the judges' marks and the results of each round, also before they are published. | [Scrutineer](/scrutineer/) |
| **Speaker** | The MC or floor manager | The current round, lineups and heats, the judges' status, the prize ceremony, and what the screens show. | [Screens, projectors and venue technology](/manager-guide/screens/) |
| **Screens** | Whoever runs the TVs and projectors | The displays in the hall: lineups, heats, results, schedule, sponsors. | [Screens, projectors and venue technology](/manager-guide/screens/) |
| **Lineup** | The person at the floor entrance | Calling the next heat and marking who is on the floor. | [Users, apps and stations](/manager-guide/users-and-stations/) |
| **Check-in** | The check-in desk and the door | Finding dancers, scanning Vote4Dance IDs and tickets, start numbers, arrivals, selling at the desk. | [At the door](/manager-guide/the-door/) |
| **Organizer** | The person who sells to the audience | The event's shop: products, orders and admission. | [Shop](/manager-guide/shop/) |
| **Cafe** | The cafe counter | Products and the live order queue. | [Shop](/manager-guide/shop/#the-café) |
| **Music (DJ)** | The DJ | The dancers' own tracks, to listen to and download. | [Music from the dancers](/manager-guide/music/) |

## Notifications

Organizers send event updates as push notifications from Manager, see
[Notifications](/manager-guide/notifications/). You choose which ones you receive in your own
settings: **Settings → Notifications** in the app, or **Account → Notification preferences** on
the website. Results for teams you have favorited and updates for competitions you follow can be
switched off separately. Both are on by default. E-mails, such as registration confirmations,
are always sent.
