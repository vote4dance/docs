# Get the app

The Vote4Dance app is for iPhone and Android. You do not need it to register, buy a ticket or
see results: that all works on the website. The app is the handy way to follow a competition on
the day, and it is where the audience gives hearts and plays the quiz.

| Your phone | Get it here |
|---|---|
| iPhone | [Vote4Dance on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/vote4dance/id1050637639) |
| Android | [Vote4Dance on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.vote4dance) |

Then sign in with the same e-mail address you use on the website, and a code sent to it. New to
Vote4Dance? Choose **Join Vote4Dance** on the first screen. See [Signing in](/signing-in/).
