# Release notes
## 2026-11-19
 ⭐ **New**
* Scrutineer (Beta): **Let autopilot do it** on a round in **Live** lets autopilot run just that round, so you can try it without switching it on for the whole event. A short window says what autopilot will do in the round and lets you choose whether it confirms after the countdown or waits for you to press Confirm. When autopilot has run a round, the class's next round offers **Run the … on autopilot** or **Whole class from here…** (see [Let autopilot run a round](/manager-guide/live-operations/#let-autopilot-run-a-round))
 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Manager: A final made of a gold battle and a bronze battle that starts from 3rd now places its dancers 1–4 from the two battles, also when the final itself is judged as battles, and a final with three dancers (a gold battle and a 3rd place) places them 1–3. Before, such a final gave the gold battle's winner 1st and the other three 2nd until its judging was switched to placement. The round page now also marks the bronze battle's winner with an **x**, as it does the gold battle's (see [Final with a bronze battle and a gold battle](/federation-rules/battle-sub-rounds/#setup-2--final-with-a-bronze-battle-and-a-gold-battle))
* Manager: In **Update/import participants** and a file import, you can now untick the new classes in the preview together with the participants in them, and the rest is imported. If a ticked participant is in a class you unticked, nothing is imported and a message says to tick the class or untick the participant. Before, the import failed with "Class Not Found", and classes it had already added stayed (see [Update/import participants](/manager-guide/registration-checkin/#updateimport-participants))
* Federation: Moving a competitor's class membership on **Members** to a class they had a retired membership in now works. The old membership becomes the active one with your changes, and the one you edited is retired. If the competitor is still active in that class, you see "This competitor already has an active membership in that class". Before, saving showed "Internal Error" (see [Editing a membership](/federation/members/#editing-a-membership))
* Manager and Scrutineer (Beta): A couple you sent through or kept out by hand in a closed round (**Changed by hand**) now stays as you set it when you change something else on the same couple afterwards, such as its **Through** or **Alt** or its battle. Before, such a change took the mark away, and confirming the round set the couple from the result again, which could send one couple too many to the next round (see [Changing who goes through by hand](/manager-guide/live-operations/#changing-who-goes-through-by-hand))
## 2026-11-18
 🛠️ **Improvements**
* App and website: Searching for a dancer or start number now shows the class the entry is in after the organizer moves it to another class, by the class's full name. Before, the search kept showing the class the entry was first entered in
* Manager: The **Class** column on check-in and in the check-in report now shows the class the entry is in, by its full name. Before, it showed the class from the import, which could be out of date after a class change or empty
## 2026-11-18
 🛠️ **Improvements**
* Website: On a phone, the division cards on a federation's **Divisions** page now show **Season ranking** and **Explore** under the division's name. Before, the name was squeezed to a few letters per line
* Website: A division's image now shows on its card on the federation's **Divisions** page, also when it was added before March 2026. Before, those cards stayed black. Images added back then were saved small, so upload them again for a sharp card (see [Add a division](/federation/divisions/#add-a-division))
## 2026-11-16
 ⭐ **New**
* Website: A federation that publishes its season ranking now has **Season ranking** in its menu, next to **Divisions**, opening the ranking of each published division, and a **Season ranking** button next to **Explore** on those divisions. On a phone the federation menu is a row you can swipe instead of folding away (see [The public season ranking](/federation/season-ranking/#the-public-season-ranking))
* Registration: A class the team's season points have closed now has a **Closed** tag, and the reason names the level, such as "From 18 points, it can no longer enter Rising Talent". The team's points this season show next to the team picker, a note under the list says that entries made before the team reached the limit stay, and **See the season ranking** opens the federation's ranking when it publishes one (see [A class closed by your season points](/dancer/registration-for-event/#a-class-closed-by-your-season-points))
* App: **Ranking and progression** on your public profile now shows each of your teams' season points in federations that close a class at points: the points, a bar to the next limit with "2 points to go · from 18 you dance Ultimate Talent", and "From the next competition you dance …" once a team has reached it. Tap a row to see the results behind the total and which ones count (see [Your season points in the app](/dancer/season-points/))
* App: In a class's results each team's place is followed by the points it earned there, such as **+7**, and your own and followed teams get a line with today's points, the season total and how far they are from the limit (see [Points in the class results](/dancer/season-points/#points-in-the-class-results))
* App: When a competition's results are published and one of your teams has reached a points limit, its dancers and their parents get a message that the team moves up, and the club's coaches get one message for all the club's teams that moved up. **My teams move up a class** and **My club's teams move up a class** in the app's and the website's notification settings switch them off (see [When a team moves up](/dancer/season-points/#when-a-team-moves-up))
* Manager: **Can't judge a class…** on a judge's row on the **Panels** tab swaps a judge out of one class's rounds still to come, for example when their student dances in it, and seats the suggested judge or one you choose instead. Only that class changes: its rounds get a copy of their panel marked **(substitute)**, rounds with scores keep the judge, and the class can be added to the judge's **Classes not to judge** at the same time. A **Must not judge** problem in a round still to come offers **Swap out…** for the same (see [A judge who can't judge one class](/manager-guide/judges-on-the-day/#a-judge-who-cant-judge-one-class))
* Competitors: With **Favorites** picked, **List** shows a short list of the clubs and dancers you follow: one row per start number with the dancers, their club and every class they are entered in, with the round each class starts in. Sort it by start number or by club, and **Print** it or save it as a PDF in portrait or landscape (see [List your club's entries](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#list-your-clubs-entries))
* Schedule: With **Favorites** picked, the schedule is now a timetable of the clubs and dancers you follow. Each of their rounds lists your dancers with start number, name and club, later rounds show who got through, and the other rounds stay greyed out or fold away with **Only followed**. **Print** prints the day's timetable, in portrait or landscape, or saves it as a PDF, for team leaders and coaches who want it on paper (see [Find your club's rounds](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#find-your-clubs-rounds))
* Results: A class run as battles can be read as a **Tree** on the event page, next to **Tables**. It shows the selection round and every battle round, with each battle's dancers, votes and the judges who voted, who went on, the B-final and the placings. On a phone you move between rounds one at a time (see [Battle results as a tree](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#battle-results-as-a-tree))
* App: A class run as battles opens on **Tree** in the app's result sheet too, next to **Tables**: one round at a time, with each battle's dancers, the judges who voted, the votes and where the winner goes next, and the final, B-final and placings on the last page (see [In the Vote4Dance app](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#in-the-vote4dance-app))
* Speaker: The **Results** page lists every battle of a battle round, live while it runs, with its status, the winner, the score, the judges' votes and a **Read out** line, and shows who meets whom in the next round so the next battle can be called up early (see [Battles in the speaker app](/manager-guide/screens/#battles-in-the-speaker-app))
* Scrutineer (Beta): **Print judge schedules** on the Judges list prints one slip per judge, two to an A4 page, with each class they judge, the estimated time of its first round, the number of starters and the number of rounds, and a QR code to their schedule in the Judging app. **Print schedule** in a judge's **⋯** menu prints that judge alone. For events over several days you print one day or all days, and at an event with several competitions **Whole event** gives each judge one slip per day across all of them (see [Printing the schedules](/manager-guide/judge-schedules/#printing-the-schedules) and [Events over several days](/manager-guide/judge-schedules/#events-over-several-days))
* Judging: While there is nothing to judge, the Judging app shows **Your next round**, **Today** and **My schedule**: every round the judge sits on, with finished rounds marked **done** and the next one highlighted. At an event over several days it opens at today, with earlier days folded away. Before, the screen only said "Great job! Waiting for a round..." (see [In the Judging app](/manager-guide/judge-schedules/#in-the-judging-app))
* Manager: Invitation and **Notify** emails to a judge with classes to judge now list **Your classes**, with the first-round times and starters, and an active judge's button reads **Open my schedule** (see [In the invitation email](/manager-guide/judge-schedules/#in-the-invitation-email))

* Website: Red warning text, such as the reason a class is locked, is now a darker red that is easier to read on a white background. Before, it was a light pink
* App: In a class's **Tree**, a battle won over several rounds now shows a dot for each round next to the rounds won, filled for a round the dancer won, a ring for one they lost, as on the website. Before, the app showed only the number (see [In the Vote4Dance app](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#in-the-vote4dance-app))

It gets no place, and the results file shows it as withdrawn (**S**). Before, it stayed in the round, was placed last, and the results file gave it that place (see [A dancer withdraws before the first round](/manager-guide/live-operations/#a-dancer-withdraws-before-the-first-round))
* Competitors: After you click a club under **Organizations**, **Download CSV** now shows at the top next to **All** and **Favorites**, with the club's name. Before, it sat at the bottom of the class list (see [Download a club's entries as a spreadsheet](/spectator/following-the-schedule/#download-a-clubs-entries-as-a-spreadsheet))
* Speaker: On **Lineup** and **Live**, a final or B-final that hasn't been drawn yet now shows the dancers already through as soon as a battle feeding it is confirmed, such as "Winner of Battle semi 1", with a "?" and "Waiting for Battle semi 2" for the place still to be danced for. Before, both said there were no dancers yet until the whole round was published, while the secretariat already saw the names (see [Who is already through, on Lineup](/manager-guide/screens/#who-is-already-through-on-lineup))
* Manager: When you send a round's participants on, **Round for those that qualify** warns when the round you picked skips a round, for example "Preliminary sent its qualifiers to Quarterfinal. This sends them to Semifinal instead." for a second chance round. It's only a warning, so you can still skip a round on purpose (see [Check where the round sends its participants](/manager-guide/live-operations/#check-where-the-round-sends-its-participants))
* Scrutineer (Beta): **Add a single round** can now put a round between two rounds with **Place in class** → **After** and a round, for example a quarterfinal after the preliminary. The round before then sends its qualifiers to the new round, and the new round sends them on. Deleting a round links the round before it to the round after it. Before, a new round always went after the final, so no round sent anyone to it (see [Add a round between two rounds](/manager-guide/rounds-by-hand/#add-a-round-between-two-rounds))
* Manager: A round that has no round set for its qualifiers now sends them past its own second chance round to the next round when it starts. Before, they could be sent into the second chance round
* Speaker: On **Live** and **Lineup**, when rounds on a shared floor show side by side, each round's heats now sit under each other, or two abreast on a wide screen. Before, a round with two or three heats split its half of the screen into one narrow card per heat, and dancers' names broke one letter per line (see [Speaker and Supervisor](/manager-guide/floors/#speaker-and-supervisor))
* Check-in: The desk can scan the next dancer straight after pressing **Arrived**, and the arrival is still saved, with a short message naming the dancer. Moving on from an admitted dancer without pressing **Arrived** shows a warning with **Mark arrived** and **Open**, and an arrival that can't be saved, for example when the Wi-Fi drops, stays on screen with the dancer's name and **Try again**. Before, an arrival could be lost when the next dancer was scanned quickly (see [Working through a queue](/manager-guide/the-door/#working-through-a-queue))
* App: You can now claim past results entered with a member number that isn't yours, such as a number from an organizer's own system, even when you have a licence with a different number or the result has no birth year. You can claim it as long as no other dancer holds that number and its birth year, if it has one, is the one on your account. Such a result shows **Check this is you** and starts unticked, so tick only the ones that are yours. Before, these results didn't show up under **Claim past results** at all (see [If something is missing or looks wrong](/dancer/creating-and-getting-a-ranking/#if-something-is-missing-or-looks-wrong))
* Scrutineer (Beta): Couples you send on or keep out by hand in a round's **Result** tab now go as you set them when you confirm the round. Before, confirming could set every couple again from the result, and when the round had no more couples than go through, all of them went on (see [Changing who goes through by hand](/manager-guide/live-operations/#changing-who-goes-through-by-hand))
* Results: In a round danced in several styles or dances, such as a battle selection, each style's own table now heads its place column **Plac in** and the style's name, for example **Plac in New Style**. The number there is the place in that style alone; letters such as **Å** show who went on from the whole round, and the round's total table, headed **Plac**, gives the place that counts. Point at the heading to see this explained. Before, every table said **Plac**, so a dancer placed 3rd in one style could look 3rd in the round
* Speaker: The **Judges** card on **Overview** can now show the judges **By panel**: each panel with the judges seated on it, and the judges on no panel under **Not in a panel**. **All judges** shows one list per competition, as before (see [Panels in the speaker app](/manager-guide/judge-panels/#panels-in-the-speaker-app))
* Manager: Check-in and **Statistics** now show the full name of every club. A club whose dancers only dance with partners from other clubs showed only its short name, such as `OLAND`, because its name was only taken from couples where both dancers are from that club (see [Organization check-in controls](/manager-guide/registration-checkin/#organization-check-in-controls))
* Manager: On an event whose entries you imported, without a registration period, **Set up payment** in the event checklist is now **Optional** and no longer the next step, and the overview no longer says sign-up is waiting for payment. Before, the checklist kept pointing you to it (see [Organizations sign their dancers' entries](/manager-guide/registration-checkin/#organizations-sign-their-dancers-entries))
