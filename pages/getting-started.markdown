# Start here

Vote4Dance runs dance competitions: registration, check-in, judging, results and rankings. What
you need from these pages depends on what you do at a competition.

## Find your section

| You… | Go to |
|---|---|
| set up and run a competition | [Organizer](/organizer/) |
| compete, or your child does | [Dancer](/dancer/) |
| run a club, school or team and register its dancers | [School/Club](/school-club/) |
| come to watch | [Spectator](/spectator/) |
| work for a federation: licenses, classes, rules, rankings | [Federation](/federation/) |
| oversee judging and results at an event | [Scrutineer](/scrutineer/) |

Many people are more than one of these: a club administrator who also dances, a federation
official who also organizes a championship. Each section stands on its own, so read the one for
the task in front of you.

## How it fits together

- A **federation** sets the rules: which classes exist, who needs a license, how points and
  rankings work. Not every competition has one.
- An **organizer** creates an **event** and runs it. An event holds one or more competitions,
  each with classes and rounds. It can follow a federation's rules or stand alone.
- A **club** (school, studio, team) has members, and can register them for events and handle
  their licenses.
- A **dancer** has one account, joins a club if they have one, registers for events, and finds
  their results, points and rankings afterwards.

At the event itself, the organizer runs each round, the judges mark it, and the results are
published to the screens in the hall, the website and the app.
